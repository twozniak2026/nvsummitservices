Thankful for Community: Education Materials

Friday, November 13, 2026 · BELFOR Restoration · Reno, Nevada

Topics from the November 2026 presentation

This resource guide accompanies the 33-page November 2026 Education presentation from the Thankful for Community summit.

  • Nevada HOA laws and governance: NRS 116, NAC 116, NRS 116A, NAC 116A, nonprofit corporation law, governing documents, board duties, transparency and meetings.
  • Financial governance and collections: budgets, independent financial reviews or audits, purchasing and bidding, collection policies and Nevada’s collection process.
  • Construction and community preparedness: Juniper Ridge Construction; Fire Hard’s defensible-space and wildfire-preparedness presentation.
  • Reserve studies: statutory review and update requirements, components, 30-year funding forecasts, full, threshold and baseline funding approaches, and a case study for board discussion.
  • Association finances: Western Alliance Association Bank’s session about HOA loans, funding options and FDIC coverage.
  • Interactive education: HOA Jeopardy, including a final round on reserve-study funding schedules.

Referenced Nevada legal resources

NRS Chapter 116 · NAC Chapter 116 · NRS Chapter 116A · NAC Chapter 116A
